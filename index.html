Security+ certified in July 2026. Below is where the certification turns into something running — one tool that detects, one assessment that governs.

authwatch

SSH authentication log analysis and detection engine

Parses raw auth.log files into structured events, runs four detection rules against them, and stores findings in SQLite behind a REST API and dashboard. Built to answer one question: given a log nobody has time to read, what in it actually deserves a human's attention?

$ python -m authwatch ingest data/auth.log
Parsed 83 events from data/auth.log
Stored 83 new events, 5 new alerts

[HIGH ] brute_force        10 failed logins for 'root' from 203.0.113.42 within 10 minutes
[HIGH ] password_spray     198.51.100.7 attempted 5 distinct accounts within 30 minutes
[MEDIUM] off_hours_success  Successful login for 'aiden' at 21:06
[MEDIUM] new_ip_success     First success from previously unseen IP 45.77.201.9

The two HIGH alerts are failed attacks. The two MEDIUM alerts share a timestamp and an IP — a successful login, outside business hours, from an address that had never authenticated before. Neither rule rates that high alone. Together they are the finding worth investigating.
